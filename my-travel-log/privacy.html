Privacy Policy

My Travel Log by FelixAnalytix — last updated 2 August 2026

The short version. My Travel Log has no server and no database. Your travel data is stored in your own browser, and — only if you choose it — in a single file inside your own Google Drive. It is never sent to us, and we have no technical means of reading it. We run no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

My Travel Log is a free, open-source travel-map application published by FelixAnalytix (Félix Luginbühl). The source code is public, so every claim on this page can be checked against it. Contact: felix.luginbuhl@protonmail.ch.

What the app stores, and where

My Travel Log is local-first. You can use it fully without an account. Everything you enter is kept in your browser.

1. In your browser (always)

WhatWhereWhy
Visited countries, trips (city, coordinates, dates, transport), display preferences Cookies travellog_countries, travellog_trips, travellog_prefs, with a localStorage fallback. Expire after one year. So the app works offline and your data survives a reload.
Small interface flags: your storage choice, whether you dismissed a prompt, whether you have exported a backup localStorage So the app respects choices you already made and stops asking.

These are functional, not analytics. They are never transmitted anywhere, and there are no advertising, profiling or third-party tracking cookies of any kind.

2. In your Google Drive (only if you choose it)

If you choose to sync, the app writes one file, travellog.json, to your Google Drive. It contains exactly what you entered: your visited countries, your trips, and your display preferences.

The app requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This grants access only to files the app itself created. My Travel Log cannot see, open, list or modify any other file in your Google Drive — this is enforced by Google, not merely promised by us.

Because the file is a normal, visible file in your Drive, you can open it, read it, copy it or delete it yourself at any time. The Settings page links directly to it.

3. Your email address

When you are connected, the app asks Google for the email address of the Drive account you connected, purely to display it in Settings so you can tell which account is in use. It is held in memory for that browser session only. It is not stored, not written to your Drive file, and not transmitted to us or to anyone else. If Google does not provide it, the app simply shows "Connected" instead.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server, no database, no logs and no analytics. We do not use Google Analytics or any equivalent. We cannot see how many people use the app, who they are, or what they have entered. If you email us, we will of course have your email — that is all.

Third parties the app contacts

The app is a static web page. When it runs in your browser, it makes requests to the following services. Each receives your IP address, as is unavoidable for any web request.

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Google (accounts.google.com, googleapis.com) Only if you choose to sync Your sign-in, and the contents of your travellog.json, which is stored in your own Google account. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
Photon (photon.komoot.io), an open-source OpenStreetMap geocoder As you type in the city search box The text you type. This happens whether or not you sync, and it is the one piece of what you type that leaves your device.
flagcdn.com When flag images are displayed Which flag images are requested — which indirectly indicates which countries you have marked as visited.
GitHub Pages / our host When the page loads A standard web request for the page itself.

The world map itself is drawn from country outlines built into the page, so no map-tile server is contacted and your map view is not sent anywhere.

Sharing

The Share button produces a public link containing only your visited three-letter country codes and your chosen map colour — for example #/w?c=CHEFRAITA&k=0f766e. No trips, cities, dates, email or account identifier is included. Anyone with the link sees only a coloured world map. Such a link cannot be revoked, and it is a snapshot: countries you add later will not appear in a link you already shared.

Google API Services — Limited Use

My Travel Log's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Concretely: data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide the travel-log features you requested. It is never transferred to us or to third parties, never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by humans.

Your control

  • Use the app without syncing at all. Choosing "This device only" means nothing ever leaves your browser, and the app will not contact Google.
  • Export your data as a JSON file at any time from Settings.
  • Delete the Drive copy from Settings ("Stop syncing and delete from Drive"), or delete travellog.json yourself in Google Drive.
  • Delete the local copy with "Clear all travel data" in Settings, or by clearing your browser data.
  • Revoke our access entirely, at any time and without involving us, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and no request you need to send us to make deletion happen.

Data retention

We retain no personal data, so there is no retention period. Your browser keeps its copy until you clear it; your Google Drive keeps its copy until you delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Since the app is open source, the history of this file is public too.

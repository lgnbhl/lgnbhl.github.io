My Travel Log

by FelixAnalytix

My Travel Log is a free web app for keeping track of the countries you have visited and the trips you have taken. You mark countries on a world map, add trips with a city and dates, and the app shows you statistics about your travels — how many countries and continents you have been to, how many days you have spent travelling, and what share of the world you have seen.

Open My Travel Log Privacy policy

What you can do

  • Map — tap any country on a world map to mark it visited.
  • Trips — record a city, dates and how you travelled (plane, train, car or boat), and see upcoming and past trips.
  • Stats — countries, cities, continents, total days travelled, and the percentage of the world's countries you have visited.
  • Share — produce a public link showing only your coloured world map, with no trips, cities or dates in it.

Where your data is stored

No account is required and there is no sign-in wall. Open the app and start using it immediately — everything you enter is saved in your own browser.

If you would like your travel log to survive clearing your browser and to follow you to other devices, you can optionally connect your own Google Drive. The app then keeps a single file, travellog.json, in your Drive. It uses Google's drive.file permission, which grants access only to files the app itself created — it cannot see anything else in your Drive. My Travel Log has no server and no database, so your travel data is never sent to us and we have no way to read it.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

How it is built

My Travel Log is a client-side single-page application: it runs entirely in your browser, with no server of its own. It is built in R with the reactLeaflet, muiMaterial and reactRouter packages — all three of which are open source. The world map is drawn from country outlines built into the page, so it needs no map-tile server.
